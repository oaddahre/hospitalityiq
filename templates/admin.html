Coordinate corrections (this server's hotels.csv)

Upload a reviewed coordinate_changes.csv from scripts/coordinate_audit.py. Only rows marked "auto" or "yes" are applied, and only their latitude and longitude. The file is backed up first, checked after writing, and every change is logged. Rows already applied are skipped, so applying twice is safe.

Google IDs found by a lookup run elsewhere (google_ids_upload.json), so they are not paid for again. IDs this server already has are kept; nothing is overwritten.

Approve candidates: a coordinate_candidates.csv from scripts/find_candidates.py with approve = "yes" on the rows you checked (one per hotel). Each approved row sets that hotel's Google ID and moves its coordinates to Google's position. Other rows are ignored. Both files are backed up first; approving twice is safe.

Change the hotel list: a CSV with the columns hotel_id and action, optionally hotel (the current name, checked against this server's list) and reason. Actions: hide, unhide, remove, rename (new_name), city (new_city, optionally new_region), owner (new_owner), rooms (new_rooms: the room count, a whole number), brand (new_brand, new_brand_group), notes (new_notes), merge (removes a duplicate: into = the id of the hotel kept; saved comp sets switch to the kept hotel), add (a new hotel: hotel_id = its new id, hotel = its name, and new_city, new_region, new_category, new_brand, new_brand_group, new_keys, new_year_opened, new_status, new_lat, new_lng, new_owner and the other hotels.csv columns as new_…; perf_… columns add its performance.csv row; refused if the id or the name is taken or on the do-not-add list), to_pipeline (moves the hotel to the pipeline: new_name, pipeline_status, expected_opening, or pipeline_id to rename an existing project instead), pipeline_add (a project not open yet: pipeline_id, hotel = its name, new_city, new_region, new_category, new_brand, new_brand_group, new_owner, new_keys, new_expected_opening, new_status, new_investment_mad, new_lat, new_lng, new_notes; refused if the id or the name is taken), pipeline_position (pipeline_id, hotel = the project's name, new_lat, new_lng, approximate=yes if it is a town-centre estimate), pipeline_update (pipeline_id, hotel = the project's name, and the new_… values to change, e.g. new_keys, new_expected_opening, new_status, new_notes; new_converts_hotel_id marks a conversion of that operating hotel, whose keys are not counted as new rooms), pipeline_remove (pipeline_id, hotel = the project's name; into_hotel_id = the operating hotel it became, if any). Hidden hotels keep their row but are left out of every page, count, KPI and scraper run; remove deletes the row. hotels.csv and pipeline.csv are backed up first; running the same file twice changes nothing; restore a backup below to undo.

Owner profiles: an owners_upload.json ({"format": "kodo-owners-1", "owners": [...]}) adds an owner profile, or replaces the one with the same id. Owners not in the file are kept as they are. owners.json is backed up first; applying twice changes nothing; restore a backup below to undo.

Calibration runs once a month, after the first model run of the month, against the real figures in daily_performance.csv (Upload performance). It reports the average error of v1 and v2 by city and hotel type in the Rates panel; with no real figures it does nothing and says so.

Switch to v2

The platform switches each city to v2 by itself after the nightly run, once 80% of rooms are in ready hotels and the city meets the rules below; hotels and cities not ready stay on v1. Before each switch the city's figures are stored here and a summary is emailed to admin@kodohospitality.com. The kill switch (or OCC_V2_OFF=1 in Railway) returns every hotel to v1 at once.

v2 readiness

What v2 needs to run on real data only. A hotel: its own normal price (14+ priced nights, no stand-in reference price), 28+ days of price collection, prices on 4 of the last 7 days. A city: 3+ ready hotels, 60% of its open hotels ready, a market signal (3+ priced hotels) on 80% of the next 30 nights. Suggested switch: 80% of rooms in ready hotels. Dates are projected from the pace of price collection so far.

Official monthly occupancy, by city

Each city's hotels are held to the official occupancy of classified accommodation for the month (room-weighted average of their nights): published months as published; a year earlier rebuilt from the published change (rounded points); a month missing from a page from its region; months with no page between the nearest months; later months the latest year moved by the city's yearly change (at most ±5 pts). Cities with their own monthly pattern use it as their seasonality, and resorts there keep no separate resort swing. Lower and medium confidence mark a region standing in for a city, or a destination that may not match our city exactly. "months" cities: only their published months are held. Clients see "Kodo est., calibrated on official monthly city occupancy" only for the cities with a pattern. New pages (e.g. February and August 2026): add official_monthly_occupancy_<month>.csv in the same format next to official_monthly_occupancy.csv and deploy; its months replace the filled ones. Ranges: by hotel type (city 14, resort 20, other 24 pts each side), +3 where a region stands in, +5 with no monthly series. Source (internal only): —.

Resort seasonal swing, by destination

Classified beach and seaside resorts follow a seasonal swing — times the city pattern where it is switched on: "measured" where hotel-reported figures support it, "lower confidence" on the same coast and summer market without figures yet. Elsewhere resorts keep their city's pattern. Railway settings: RESORT_SEASON_ON / RESORT_SEASON_OFF (destination names, comma-separated). Price position: — (PRICE_POSITION: off, nonresort or on).
