01

Data Sources

What we measure and where it comes from — Kōdō combines a curated hotel directory, daily rate scraping, a statistical occupancy model, and direct operator data partnerships.

Hotel Directory High Confidence

Kōdō maintains a manually curated database of 300+ branded and independent hotels across 20+ Moroccan destinations. Each property is verified for existence, key count, segment classification, brand affiliation, and ownership. Data quality is flagged at the property level — verified entries have been confirmed against official hotel websites, brand press kits, and industry sources. Unverified entries are clearly marked.

Rate Intelligence High Confidence

Starting July 2026 Kōdō scrapes Best Available Rates (BAR) daily for all properties in our database using a multi-source approach: Google Hotels API (primary), brand direct websites (secondary), and OTA platforms (fallback). BAR represents the lowest publicly available rate for a standard room, 2 adults, 1 night. BAR is not equivalent to a hotel's actual Average Daily Rate (ADR) — it excludes negotiated corporate rates, wholesale contracts, and package inclusions — but provides a consistent, comparable daily rate signal across all properties.

Occupancy Model Medium Confidence

Kōdō does not have access to real occupancy data from hotel PMS systems except for properties participating in our Data Sharing Program. For all other properties we estimate occupancy using a statistical model with the following inputs: base occupancy by market segment and hotel type; rate index adjustment; day-of-week patterns; monthly seasonality index derived from ONMT national tourism data; OTA availability signals where captured; and a major events calendar. The model produces a point estimate with a confidence interval.

Verified Operator Data Highest Confidence

Hotels participating in the Kōdō Data Sharing Program share daily operational data (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR) directly from their Property Management System. This data is anonymised before inclusion in any benchmarking output and is never disclosed at the individual property level. Verified data is displayed with a "Verified" badge and is used to calibrate our statistical model, improving estimates for all properties over time.

Asset Value Estimation

Kōdō estimates indicative asset values using revenue multiples by segment — a standard methodology in hotel real estate. These multiples represent the relationship between annual rooms revenue and total asset value, calibrated against available Morocco transaction data and regional benchmarks.

04

Understanding Our Data Quality Labels

Every property and metric on the Kōdō platform carries a data quality label. These are not cosmetic — they indicate the source and reliability of the underlying data.

Verified

Data confirmed against official sources — brand press kits, official hotel websites, government tourism records, or direct operator communication. Key count, opening year, brand affiliation, and ownership have been individually verified.

Kōdō Estimate

Performance metrics (occupancy, ADR, RevPAR) estimated using our statistical model. Suitable for market-level analysis and relative benchmarking. Not verified against operator PMS data. Always labelled clearly in platform displays and reports.

Key Count Uncertain

Hotel existence is confirmed but the precise room count has not been independently verified. Key count shown is our best estimate based on available sources including brand materials, planning records, and press coverage.

05

What We Don't Claim

Transparency about limitations is a core part of our methodology. The following points should be understood by all users of Kōdō data.

  • Kōdō estimates are not a substitute for operator-verified PMS data. For investment underwriting, always seek actual operator performance data.
  • BAR (Best Available Rate) is not equivalent to ADR (Average Daily Rate). BAR excludes negotiated corporate rates, loyalty redemptions, wholesale contracts, and package inclusions, which can represent 30–60% of a hotel's actual revenue mix.
  • Occupancy estimates carry a margin of error of approximately ±8–12 percentage points for properties without verified real data. Estimates are more accurate for markets with higher data density and longer observation periods.
  • Financial estimates — asset values, revenue ranges, and RevPAR figures — are indicative only. They reflect current market conditions and available data, and may differ materially from actual performance or appraised values.
  • All data on the Kōdō platform should be independently verified before forming the basis of investment decisions, operator negotiations, or financial reporting.
  • Kōdō Hospitality is a market intelligence provider, not a licensed financial advisor. Nothing on this platform constitutes investment advice.
06

Improving Accuracy Through Partnership

The accuracy of Kōdō's market intelligence improves with every hotel that contributes real operational data. Properties in our Data Sharing Program share daily occupancy, ADR, and RevPAR from their PMS. This data is anonymised and aggregated — individual hotel figures are never disclosed to other subscribers.

In return, participating hotels receive free Benchmarker access — our full competitive intelligence suite including anonymous comp set benchmarking, trend charts, and monthly summaries — at no cost, for as long as the data sharing agreement is active. Data contributors also benefit from improved model accuracy for their own market, better seasonal calibration, and early access to new platform features.

What participants receive:

  • Free Benchmarker plan access (normally MAD 2,490/month)
  • Anonymous comp set benchmarking vs. your competitive set
  • Monthly performance trend reports
  • Priority support and direct access to our research team
  • Influence over coverage expansion and new features

Data is processed under a strict confidentiality agreement. Individual property data is never disclosed in any form. Aggregated benchmarking output always includes a minimum of 3 properties per disclosure, per STR confidentiality standards.
