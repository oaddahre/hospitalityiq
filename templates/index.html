How it is computed: published rates and modelled estimates, not achieved performance

What this is. BAR (published rates: the prices hotels show for sale) and Kodo's occupancy estimate. Kodo has no hotel's actual sales, achieved rates or revenue, so none is shown.

Published rates. Every night from 03:00 Casablanca time Kodo collects each hotel's published prices: a one-night stay for 2 adults, no children, in MAD, for each of the next 30 nights where a price is shown. The request sets no number of rooms; the default room count applies. Each price is the nightly price returned for that night: a published rate (BAR), not what guests paid. Room type, board basis and cancellation terms are not controlled, and whether taxes and fees are included depends on the booking site. Where the rates come from and since when: see the Methodology page (link at the foot of the page).

How a night's price is chosen. The most recently collected price for that night, at most 7 days old. A price is used only if the result passed Kodo's name and city check for that hotel.

What is excluded. Results for a different hotel or city, prices older than 7 days, prices entered by hand, prices outside 200 to 60,000 MAD, and nights with no price. A night without a live price shows "–" and is left out of every average.

Rate index and rank. Rate index = your published rate ÷ the comp set's median published rate × 100, over the nights where both exist (100 = priced like the median). Rank orders your hotel among the comp set by average published rate over the next 30 nights (1 = highest).

Modelled occupancy (Kodo estimate). Modelled, not measured: a base occupancy for the hotel's type and segment, adjusted for day of week, month, holidays and local events, and for published prices (see the Methodology page for how). Where the model gives a range it is shown as low–high. Kodo does not use "rooms left" messages. Price coverage describes how many recent published prices we have for the hotel, not how accurate the estimate is: none with no price for the night, partial with one, full with one and prices collected for the hotel on at least 4 of the last 7 days.
