Platform

Six modules. One interface.

01

National KPIs

Keys-weighted national benchmarks — occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, TRevPAR, and GOP margin — updated at the portfolio level in real time.

02

City Segmentation

Performance by city: Casablanca, Marrakech, Agadir, Fès, Rabat, Tangier, Essaouira. Compare markets across every KPI.

03

Hotel Pipeline

Track 11 confirmed projects and 1,400+ keys in development. View opening dates, investment values, brand affiliations, and pipeline concentration by city.

04

Benchmarking

Upload your own property data and benchmark performance against the Kōdō market database — by city, segment, or brand group.

05

Market Intelligence

Curated editorial on hotel openings, investment transactions, and demand trends — written by the Kōdō research team.

06

AI Analyst

Ask any question about Moroccan hotel performance and receive data-grounded answers from Kōdō's AI, trained exclusively on the platform's proprietary database.
