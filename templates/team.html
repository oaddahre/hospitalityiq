Principles

How we work

01

Data before narrative

Every claim on the Kōdō platform is traceable to a source. We don't publish market commentary that can't be grounded in actual hotel performance data. If we don't have the data, we say so.

02

Precision, not approximation

Moroccan hospitality has operated on informal estimates for too long. Kōdō applies rigorous methodology to KPI calculation — keys-weighted averages, consistent definitions, clearly labelled estimates versus verified figures.

03

Serve the operator and the investor

We design for both sides of the transaction. Operators need competitive intelligence to manage performance; investors need underwriting data to allocate capital. Kōdō is built to be useful to both without compromising either.

04

Morocco first

Kōdō is not a generic hospitality analytics tool applied to Morocco — it was built here, for this market. Our coverage decisions, methodology, and editorial voice are shaped by deep familiarity with how Moroccan hospitality actually operates.
